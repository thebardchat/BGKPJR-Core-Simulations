### Aerodynamic Recommendations

3. **Trajectory optimization** — Steeper initial climb angle reduces time spent in dense atmosphere and lowers total heat load

### Required Thermal Protection System

- **Transpiration cooling** for sustained exposure zones
- **Sacrificial ablator layer** for peak heating at tube exit transition
