# BGKPJR-Core-Simulations
Patent Application
United States Patent and Trademark Office

Title of Invention
Brazelton Gryphon Kepler Propulsion Jump Revolution (BGKPJR) Launch Architecture

Abstract
A multi-stage space launch system comprising a terrestrial magnetic levitation linear accelerator track inclined at 15 to 45 degrees, a hypersonic lifting-body spacecraft ("Gryphon"), and an orbital solar sail propulsion module ("Kepler"). The system utilizes a partial-vacuum tube to accelerate the payload to velocities exceeding Mach 3 prior to atmospheric interaction, significantly reducing the onboard chemical propellant mass fraction required for orbital insertion as defined by the Tsiolkovsky Rocket Equation.

Claim 1
A launch system wherein the initial velocity (Delta-V) is provided by ground-based electromagnetic forces rather than onboard chemical combustion, utilizing a superconducting maglev track to achieve hypersonic exit velocities.

Claim 2
A spacecraft comprising variable geometry "Gryphon" wings that remain retracted during tube acceleration to maximize bore efficiency and deploy upon exit to generate aerodynamic lift, converting ballistic trajectory into a lifting ascent.
